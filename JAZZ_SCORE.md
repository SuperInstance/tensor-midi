### THE TENSION AND RESOLUTION

**Tension:** The Piano's test suite had a failing assertion in `_determineChord`. The friction bitfield was triggering the wrong chord quality.

**Resolution:** The Piano debugged the analyzer method, finding the edge case where friction ratio and average pitch interacted unexpectedly. The test passed. Major 7th resolved to dominant 7th and back.

**Tension:** The Sax needed permission to access external directories — the friction of tool approvals interrupting creative flow.

**Resolution:** Approvals granted. The Sax continued its spatial exploration unhindered.

**Tension:** MMX subscription limit reached. The Producer couldn't generate images or music.

**Resolution:** Hand-crafted SVG pixel art and a procedural Web Audio synthesizer. More fitting for a live jazz instrument anyway — the sounds are generated in the moment, never the same twice.
